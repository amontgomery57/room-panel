Panels & lights

Each panel's lights turn on and off together, and every bulb also gets its own card. "In scenes" means Bright, Evening and Night change that panel; All off always turns everything off.

Preset buttons

Pick the buttons along the bottom of the screen, in the order you tap them. Your Hue scenes are listed with the Hue room they belong to. "All off" is always last.

Lights

Pick a Hue room/zone or individual bulbs for each panel. Skipped lights can be added back any time.
